# Third-party material

`data/table4/gsm8k_test.jsonl` is the GSM8K test split from
[openai/grade-school-math](https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math),
`grade_school_math/data/test.jsonl`. Its upstream MIT license is reproduced at
`data/table4/GSM8K_LICENSE.txt`. Selected prompts and gold answers retain the
source indices and are validated against that split. Original source-file
digests are recorded in the provenance manifest.

Model weights are not included. Obtain Qwen2.5-0.5B and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct from
their model publishers and comply with their terms. Optional Llama runners
require access under the model publisher's terms. Credentials are supplied
through the model provider's normal local authentication mechanism, not files
committed to this repository.

Python dependencies retain their upstream licenses. This release does not
introduce a new license grant for the project's own code.
